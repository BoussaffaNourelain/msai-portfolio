AI-Based Thermal Anomaly Detection for Hydrogen Storage and Distribution Safety Monitoring

This is a proposed system that would use ordinary thermal cameras and two connected AI models to catch early hydrogen leaks in storage tanks, pipes, and valves — one model finds the equipment in the image, the other flags small temperature changes that don't match normal behavior. I wrote the concept: the motivation, the two-stage detection approach, and why hydrogen's tendency to warm (not cool) as it expands makes this workable where regular gas-leak cameras don't work for hydrogen. This is a proposal stage, not a built or tested system yet — the output so far is the written plan itself, prepared for possible funding consideration.
