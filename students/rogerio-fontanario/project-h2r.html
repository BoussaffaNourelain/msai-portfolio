Technological Qualification of Hydrogen Micro-Addition in Offshore Diesel Engines: The Case of the H2R System

This is a published paper about qualifying the H2R system, which adds small amounts of hydrogen into offshore diesel engines to lower fuel use and emissions. I was part of the Protium Dynamics engineering team behind the system this paper qualifies, whose hardware and software work fed into the tests described. The tests showed lower diesel consumption and emissions with no engine damage, and the system reached a formal safety approval from DNV.
