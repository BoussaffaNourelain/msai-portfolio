Modernization Program for Eldercare Facilities

My real professional endeavor: upgrading assisted-living and long-term care facilities for safety, accessibility, and efficiency.

Many eldercare facilities in the United States still operate with outdated layouts, narrow corridors, insufficient lighting, and infrastructure that was never designed for residents with physical or cognitive limitations. These conditions raise fall risk, create regulatory exposure, and lower quality of life.

I combined my background in architecture, project management, and real estate into a single method covering four areas:

  • Diagnostic & evaluation
    Site visits, risk reports, and improvement plans
  • Design & renovation planning
    ADA-compliant redesign, circulation, lighting, bathrooms
  • Project execution & oversight
    Budget, schedule, and contractor coordination
  • Strategic solutions & training
    Preventive-maintenance and compliance workshops for staff

Starting in South Florida, the goal is a repeatable approach that measurably lowers fall risk and operating costs for care facilities.
