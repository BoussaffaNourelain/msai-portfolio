Current Projects

Modernization Program for Eldercare Facilities

A structured program to upgrade assisted-living and long-term care facilities for safety, ADA accessibility, and operational efficiency. I combined my background in architecture, project management, and real estate into one method covering facility diagnostics, ADA-compliant redesign, construction oversight, and staff training workshops. The result is a repeatable approach aimed at measurably lowering fall risk and operating costs, beginning with facilities in South Florida.

AI Fall-Risk Mapping for Care Facilities

A tool that studies a facility's floor plans and walkthrough photos and marks the zones most likely to cause falls, such as dim lighting, narrow corridors, and missing grab bars. I collected and labeled facility images, trained an image-recognition model, and produced a colour-coded risk map for each floor. It turns a site visit into a ranked list of accessibility fixes.

ADA Compliance Assistant

A question-and-answer assistant that checks renovation plans against ADA and Older Americans Act requirements. I gathered the regulations into a searchable knowledge base and connected it to a language model that answers design questions and flags compliance gaps, making compliance checks faster during the design stage.
