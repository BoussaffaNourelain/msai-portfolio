AI Fall-Risk Mapping for Care Facilities

A concept from my MS in AI coursework: a tool that would study a facility's floor plans and walkthrough photos and mark the zones most likely to cause falls — dim lighting, narrow corridors, missing grab bars. The plan is to label facility images, train an image-recognition model, and produce a colour-coded risk map per floor. No dataset or working prototype exists yet.
